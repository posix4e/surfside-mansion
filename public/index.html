What's included

Everything covered. Nothing owed.

This residency costs the artist nothing. While you're with us, the practical side of life is our job, so your energy can go into your work.

Housing

A room of your own in our home by the water, with space to spread out and work. Come as you are — the house is ready.

Food

Meals are on us — shared family dinners when you want company, a stocked kitchen when you'd rather cook or graze on your own schedule.

Transportation

We cover getting you here and getting you around — to town, to the beach, to wherever on the island your curiosity points.

Stipend

A regular stipend, so saying yes to this doesn't mean putting your finances on hold. Materials, coffees, small adventures — covered.

What we ask in return

A little of your creative time, shared with a very small artist

This is not a teaching job, and we're not hiring a nanny. Our daughter is two. What we're asking for is under twenty hours a week of relaxed, playful creative time with her and with us — the kind of making that needs no lesson plan.

In practice it looks like finger painting on the porch, building odd little sculptures in the backyard, drumming on pots, making up stories at the kitchen table. No curriculum, no milestones, no performance. You'd simply be one more artist in her daily life — and in ours.

We'll work the schedule around your practice, not the other way around. Deep in a piece? We'll flex. That's the whole spirit of the thing.

A typical week

Loose by design

No two weeks look the same, and that's the point. But most settle into a rhythm something like this.

Mornings

Studio time

The house is quiet, the light comes in off the water, the coffee is hot. This is your deep-work time, and we protect it.

Some afternoons

Family art time

An hour or two of paint, cardboard, mud, and stories with a delighted two-year-old collaborator. Then it's yours again.

Weekends

Explore the island

Surf beaches, rainforest trails, old towns, roadside food that ruins you for everywhere else. Go see it — we'll point the way.

Evenings

Rest

Sunsets, long dinners or long silences, a book on the porch, the sound of the sea doing its thing. Nobody will schedule you.

Why Puerto Rico

The island does half the work

The light here is famous for a reason. It's warm year-round, the sea is a short walk away, and the island runs on color — painted towns, rainforest greens, produce stands, music drifting out of open doors. It's hard to be here and not want to make something.

Puerto Rico has a deep, living culture of makers: painters, musicians, poets, cooks, builders. Between the coast, the mountains, and San Juan, there's more to draw from than any one stay could use up.

And it's easy to reach — for U.S.-based artists, no passport is required. You step off the plane and you're already somewhere else entirely.

We're not looking for a babysitter, and we're not looking for a teacher. We're looking for one more artist at the table — someone who still remembers that making things is a kind of play.

— The Surfside Mansion Family
Questions, answered honestly

Frequently asked

What does the residency cost?

Nothing. Housing, food, and transportation are covered, and we pay you a stipend on top. There's no application fee, no deposit, and no fine print — the family time described on this page is the whole exchange.

How long can I stay?

There's no fixed length. Admissions are rolling and open-ended — some artists might come for a few weeks, others for a season. Tell us what timing would serve your work and we'll figure it out together.

What kinds of artists are welcome?

All disciplines — painters, sculptors, writers, musicians, photographers, printmakers, dancers, anyone with a real practice. The only requirement is that you genuinely enjoy the idea of sharing loose, messy creative time with a two-year-old.

What does the family time actually look like?

Low-key and playful: finger painting, backyard projects, storytelling, music, whatever you and she invent together. It's not formal instruction and there's nothing to prepare. We schedule it flexibly around your studio time, and it always stays under twenty hours a week.

How do I apply?

Just write to us at info@fralex.art. Tell us who you are, share a few links to or images of your work, and let us know what timing you're imagining. No forms, no fees — we read everything and reply personally.
